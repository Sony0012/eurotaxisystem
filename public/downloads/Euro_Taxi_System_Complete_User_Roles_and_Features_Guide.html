EURO TAXI MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Complete User Roles, Scope of Authority, and Innermost Feature Guide (User Manual)

1. SUPER ADMIN / FLEET OWNER
role: super_admin
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): The highest authority in the Euro Taxi System with absolute, unrestricted 'God-Mode' control over all 21 modules, system security, database backups, user access rights, financial ledgers, and emergency fleet operations.
A. Pages and Modules Handled:
  • Super Admin / Owner Panel (/super-admin)
  • Executive Dashboard (/)
  • Unit Fleet Management (/units and /units/flagged)
  • Driver Management Hub (/driver-management, /banned, /terms, /debts)
  • Live GPS Fleet Tracking (/live-tracking)
  • Franchise & Decision Management (/decision-management)
  • Boundaries Remittance & Pricing Rules (/boundaries, /boundary-rules)
  • Maintenance & Spare Parts (/maintenance, /inventory-management, /spare-parts, /suppliers)
  • Metro Manila Number Coding (/coding)
  • Driver Behavior, Rewards & SOS Incidents (/driver-behavior/*)
  • Office & Administrative Expenses (/office-expenses)
  • Staff Salary & Payroll Management (/salary)
  • Predictive Analytics & Financial Intelligence (/analytics, /analytics/history)
  • Unit Profitability & AI Decision Support (/unit-profitability)
  • General Staff Directory (/staff, /staff/admin, /staff/drivers)
  • Support Desk & Driver Ticket Chat (/support-center)
  • Announcements & Fleet Advisories (/announcements)
  • System Activity & Security Audit Logs (/activity-logs)
  • Master Archive Vault (/archive)
  • My Account & Security Profile (/my-account)
B. Innermost Features & Exclusive Capabilities (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • Super Admin Control Center: Has 6 dedicated management tabs (Overview, Users, Audit History, Classifications, Roles, System Security).
  • Account Approvals & Rejections: Review pending employee registrations, view full profile and email OTP verification, and approve or reject accounts with 1-click.
  • Instant Account Lock / Disable: Temporarily disable or ban any user account with a customized disciplinary reason log.
  • Granular Page-Level Access Matrix: Check/uncheck permissions across 18 individual system pages for each staff account.
  • Role & Classification Customizer: Add, edit, or archive custom organizational roles and incident severity tiers (Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical).
  • Master Archive Security: Configure the master archive password required to restore or permanently purge soft-deleted records.
  • Owner Feedback Inbox: Private mailbox receiving direct suggestions, grievances, and feedback submitted by employees.
  • Emergency Vehicle Immobilizer: Remote engine kill switch to disable stolen or high-risk taxi units directly via GPS.
2. OPERATIONS MANAGER
role: manager
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): Directs daily fleet operations, driver disciplinary enforcement, operational expense approvals, franchise case decisions, and fleet financial profitability analysis.
A. Pages and Modules Handled:
  • Executive Dashboard (/)
  • Unit Management & Flagged Units (/units, /units/flagged)
  • Driver Management & Dues Breakdown (/driver-management, /banned, /terms, /debts)
  • Live GPS Tracking Hub (/live-tracking)
  • Franchise & Regulatory Decision Management (/decision-management)
  • Boundaries Remittance & Boundary Rules (/boundaries, /boundary-rules)
  • Maintenance & Spare Parts (/maintenance, /inventory-management)
  • Driver Behavior & SOS Incident Management (/driver-behavior/*)
  • Office Expense Approvals (/office-expenses)
  • Predictive Analytics & Ledger History (/analytics, /analytics/history)
  • Unit Profitability & AI DSS Insights (/unit-profitability)
  • Support Center (/support-center)
  • Announcements (/announcements)
  • Activity Logs (/activity-logs)
  • My Account (/my-account)
B. Innermost Features & Functions (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • Driver Lockout & Settlement Manager: Open the Dues & Lockout Details modal for banned/suspended drivers, inspect the sticky itemized breakdown table, adjust lockout terms (Permanent Ban vs. 7-day Suspension), or restore the driver.
  • Auto-Ban Policy Configurator: Set the threshold for Overdue Unreturned Vehicle Limits (in days) to automatically trigger vehicle lockouts at shift deadlines.
  • Expense Approvals / Rejection Workflow: Review submitted office expenses, inspect attached receipts, and click Approve or Reject.
  • Franchise Hearing Board: Review LTFRB franchise applications, hearing schedules, and decide to Approve or Reject franchise cases.
  • AI Decision Support System (DSS): Generate AI-powered recommendations per vehicle to determine whether to repair, reassign, or retire units based on profit vs. maintenance cost.
  • Live Fleet Map & Remote Engine Kill: Track speeds, routes, and remotely cut vehicle engines during theft or boundary abscondment.
3. DISPATCHER
role: dispatcher
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): Manages daily vehicle road deployment, driver shift coordination, Metro Manila number coding compliance, real-time GPS tracking, and roadside emergency SOS incident response.
A. Pages and Modules Handled:
  • Executive Dashboard (/)
  • Live GPS Tracking (/live-tracking)
  • Metro Manila Number Coding Management (/coding)
  • Unit Fleet Status & Overdue Units (/units, /units/flagged)
  • Driver Behavior & Accident Reports (/driver-behavior/*)
  • Driver Support Desk (/support-center)
  • Fleet Announcements (/announcements)
  • Internal Staff Chat (/chat)
  • My Account (/my-account)
B. Innermost Features & Functions (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • Interactive GPS Fleet Radar: Real-time map displaying all active taxis with color-coded status (Moving, Idle, Offline), speed gauges, and odometer readings.
  • Number Coding Automation: Daily automated check of vehicle license plate ending numbers against Metro Manila MMDA coding rules.
  • Coding Day Reassignment & Swap: Reassign coding schedules or allocate replacement vehicles for drivers whose units are on coding day.
  • SOS Emergency Alarm Hub: Real-time alert listener that plays alarm chimes when a driver presses the mobile app SOS Panic Button with live coordinates.
  • Accident & Rescue Dispatcher: View photo submissions of road accidents, inspect damage assessments, and dispatch towing or mobile mechanics.
  • 2-Way Driver Support Messaging: Real-time chat with drivers on the road to assist with route issues, shift extensions, or technical problems.

  • Salary & Payroll Calculation Engine: Input base pay, overtime hours, holiday multipliers, and deductions for staff and mechanics.

  • Company Memo Broadcaster: Publish company advisories and pinned notices visible across all web and mobile apps.
5. CASHIER / OPERATIONS STAFF
role: staff
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): Handles front-counter cashiering, daily boundary remittance collection from drivers, driver debt installment processing, minor disciplinary logging, and spare parts stock checks.
A. Pages and Modules Handled:
  • Executive Dashboard (/)
  • Daily Boundaries & Remittance Counter (/boundaries)
  • Driver Debts & Dues Settlement (/driver-management/debts)
  • Driver Behavior Incident Logging (/driver-behavior/incidents)
  • Spare Parts Stock Inquiries (/inventory-management)
  • Internal Staff Chat (/chat)
  • Fleet Announcements (/announcements)
  • My Account (/my-account)
B. Innermost Features & Functions (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • Daily Boundary Collection Counter: Log driver remittances by selecting driver, assigned plate, target rate, and payment amount.
  • Smart Shortage & Advance Detection: Automatically flags short boundaries (unpaid remainder auto-added to debt ledger) or advance payments.
  • Digital Collection Receipt: Generates instant printable boundary remittance vouchers with timestamps and collector signatures.
  • Debt Installment Processor: Accept partial or full debt payments from drivers, with instant balance subtraction in the database.
  • Driver Incident Entry: Log minor passenger complaints, taxi cleanliness infractions, or traffic tickets.
  • Real-Time Spare Parts Stock Lookup: Search spare parts inventory to verify stock levels (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock).
6. MECHANIC / MAINTENANCE CREW
role: mechanic
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): Responsible for physical vehicle repairs, preventive maintenance service (PMS), parts requisition from inventory, and vehicle road-readiness certification.
A. Pages and Modules Handled:
  • Maintenance Job Orders (/maintenance)
  • Spare Parts Inventory & Purchasing (/inventory-management, /spare-parts, /suppliers)
  • Unit Fleet Health Inspection (/units)
  • Fleet Announcements (/announcements)
  • Internal Staff Chat (/chat)
  • My Account (/my-account)
B. Innermost Features & Functions (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • Job Order Workflow: Create repair work orders, select repair category (Engine, Brakes, Transmission, Suspension, Electrical, Body), and assign mechanics.
  • Maintenance Status Toggles: Transition repair orders across stages: Pending -> In Progress -> Quality Testing -> Completed.
  • Spare Parts Auto-Deduction Requisition: Requisition parts during repair, automatically subtracting the quantity from stock.
  • Dynamic Vector Autocorrect Search: Search parts with phonetic and fuzzy matching, view item specifications and reference photos.
  • Vehicle Health Reset: Conduct post-repair safety checks and reset the vehicle health score to 100% to remove flagged status.
7. MOBILE APP TAXI DRIVER
role: driver
Scope of Authority (Nasasakupan): Drivers operating taxi units on the road using the Android/Capacitor Mobile App ecosystem.
A. Mobile App Screens & Modules Handled:
  • Driver Dashboard & Shift Overview
  • Live Gross Earnings & Remittance History
  • Outstanding Debts & Missed Boundary Breakdown Ledger
  • Weekly Incentive & Performance Scorecard
  • Emergency SOS Panic Button & Roadside Rescue
  • Accident Reporting Module
  • 2-Way Support Ticket Chat
  • Fleet Announcements & Bulletins
  • Driver Profile & Document Clearances
B. Innermost Features & Functions (Pinaka Loob-Looban):
  • OTP Mobile Authentication: Secure mobile registration with SMS/Email OTP verification.
  • Assigned Vehicle Status: Real-time view of assigned taxi plate number, daily boundary rate, and coding schedule.
  • Itemized Dues Ledger: View exact dates, missed boundary charges, and outstanding balances.
  • Weekly Incentive Tracker: Track completed shifts to qualify for weekly driver cash bonuses.
  • One-Touch SOS Panic Button: Instantly broadcasts emergency distress signals with real-time GPS coordinates to dispatchers.
  • Accident Submission Form: Capture vehicle damage photos, log third-party details, and upload police report notes.
  • In-App 2-Way Chat: Direct messaging with dispatchers and administrative support.
